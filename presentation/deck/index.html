5.6NextCharishma · 1 min
The next paper
  1. A fresh test of the tuned shock windows on months the project has not used (2025, or the Feb–Jun months a complete return file brings back).
  2. News at the speed it is priced: time-stamped news with daily returns, in the smaller stocks where tone shows up.
  3. A complete return file: the test sample grows by 70% to 95%.
  4. A point-in-time news source: firm-level results are limited by coverage, not models.
  5. A clean NoLBERT comparison: re-adapt with an end-2019 cut, adapt MiniLM the same way, then year by year. Plus a five-factor alpha.
What we can say: nothing large is there.
What we cannot say: that nothing is there.
