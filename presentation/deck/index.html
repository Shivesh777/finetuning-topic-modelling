3.1NoLBERTFine-tuningKevin
Our NoLBERT fine-tuning
The class example uses human similarity scores and squared error. We use same-article pairs and symmetric InfoNCE. Pooling in the run: every input was padded to 128 positions with pad id 1, but the attention mask was built as token id ≠ 0, so the padding positions were attended and averaged, in training and in the 7.96% → 89.16% evaluation alike. The downstream embedding step (SentenceTransformers, 64 tokens) masks padding properly.
3.2NoLBERTFine-tuning resultsKevin
NoLBERT fine-tuning results
150 minutes is the run’s wall-clock budget: the console log shows step 6,200 at 106.6 minutes and the stop at 164.3 minutes after a stall. The example batch is headline against lead for 32 articles dated 1 September 2020, chosen by a fixed rule before scoring, with the run’s own padding regime; the original held-out pairs are not on this machine.

B3Methods
Methods
NoLBERTDeBERTa v3 base · 109M parameters · masked language modeling, 15 epochs · 30,000-token tokenizer · pretraining text 1976–1995, validation 1996.
Fine-tuningInfoNCE, temperature 0.05: two random chunks of one article form a pair; the other 31 chunks of the batch are negatives. 198,560 of 228,883 pairs seen · 6,205 steps of 32 · 150-minute budget, less than one epoch · last-layer mean pooling, max length 128.
Historical usage testsH0: equal mean log-probability of the sentence in both eras; one-sided paired t over 20 words.
Topic stabilityShare of articles keeping their topic across two seeds: NoLBERT 69%, MiniLM 67%, LDA 58%. Largest topic share: 4.0%, 4.3%, 12.7%. NoLBERT is not the most coherent split.
Turnover and costsTurnover: half the sum of absolute weight changes per period. Costs: 10 bp per unit traded.
Multiple testingMetric: net Sharpe ratio; long-only rules use benchmark-relative returns (information ratio). Benchmark: the expected maximum over 375 portfolios with no skill that move together as ours do.
Pairwise termsNet Sharpe without the pairwise terms: monthly +0.34 → +0.01, biweekly −0.33 → −0.62, weekly −0.66 → −0.24.
FinBERTSentiment model: trained on text to 2010. Topic classifier: a different model, published Dec 2022 (tweets released Sep 2022).
