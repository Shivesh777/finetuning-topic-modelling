MFE 230ZB · news attention and returns · section 3

Reshape the space: no head, no new parameters.

What the NoLBERT fine-tune does to the vectors. Each dot is one news-article chunk; a line joins two chunks of the same article.

BEFORE

NoLBERT is pretrained for masked-word prediction on 1976–1995 text. That task never required two chunks of one article to receive similar vectors, so they don’t: the space is a crowded cone and pairs sit far apart. Raw NoLBERT finds its partner chunk 8% of the time (chance 3%) — too weak to cluster into topics.

THE FINE-TUNE

InfoNCE on 198,560 same-article chunk pairs: pull each pair together, push the other 31 articles in the batch away. No head, no new parameters — the encoder’s own embedding space is what moves. 150 minutes on one RTX 3060.

AFTER

Pairs sit together and clusters emerge — the geometry spherical k-means needs. Held-out partner-finding: 8% → 89%. The adapted encoder then embeds all 1.26M articles, and the 60-topic model of section 4 is built on this space.

Temporal discipline: pairs drawn only from articles dated before 1 Sep 2020 — the encoder never read a word of test-period text.

Illustrative geometry — the dots are synthetic, not real vectors. Run: 6,205 steps · 32-pair batches · temperature 0.05.
